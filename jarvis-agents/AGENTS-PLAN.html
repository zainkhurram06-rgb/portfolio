Decision 1: how EDITH searches Google Maps

This is the big one. A city means checking roughly 600 to 1,000 listings to keep 150 to 300 good leads.

Google Places API (my pick)Google's official data: reviews, phone, address, website, open or closed. A city takes minutes. No risk to your Google or Instagram accounts. Google gives a free monthly allowance; I'll check today's pricing and set a hard spending cap before anything runs. Needs a Google Cloud key from you.
Claude in Chrome browses MapsFree on your plan. But slow (a city could take a full day or more of your Chrome), uses up your Claude limits, and Google may show a "prove you're human" check, which I can't and won't solve.
Codex web search onlyFree on your ChatGPT plan, but search results don't reliably give exact review counts or open/closed status, so the 50+ reviews rule gets shaky.

Whatever you pick, the Instagram and Facebook checks still go through Claude in Chrome and Codex, as you asked.

Decision 2: how FRIDAY sends the demo link

WhatsApp (my pick)Most business owners outside the US live on WhatsApp. Sent through Twilio, the same company as her phone number. Needs a one-time WhatsApp Business approval.
SMSWorks everywhere, nothing to approve, but easier to ignore.
BothWhatsApp first, SMS if they're not on WhatsApp.
