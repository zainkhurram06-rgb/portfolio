VisaPro · AI Social Media Analyst · 2025–2026

Filling free webinars for a US immigration law firm.

VisaPro runs free webinars with their attorneys and turns the people who show up into clients. Visa stuff is confusing and people are scared of getting it wrong, so everything had to be simple and make them trust the firm.

01

The brief

Get the right people to sign up for each webinar: students, workers and founders from a lot of different countries.

What I did

Every webinar got its own campaign. Meta ads, posts and short videos, emails, cold email and partner outreach. L-1, H-1B Cap 2026, H-1B alternatives and F-1 to H-1B, in English, Chinese and Spanish.

My part

I ran Meta. A teammate ran Google and we checked each other’s campaigns. I planned and wrote most of the content, emails and outreach.
